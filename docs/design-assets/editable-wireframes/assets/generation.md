Landscape 3:2 composition, warm natural morning light in a quiet leafy walking park, one friendly cream mixed-breed medium dog wearing a well-fitted harness and leash, relaxed body language, whole head and chest visible with comfortable crop margins, leash leading off-frame, no people faces, no text, no logo, no UI, no watermark, realistic restrained photography, muted greens and warm cream palette.
